# Limitations

- The system is designed for a campus-scale demonstration, not a production election.
- Wallet addresses are public and may be linkable to students outside the application.
- Reveal transactions expose choices during the reveal period.
- Voter registration is owner-controlled.
- Voters who lose their secret cannot reveal.
- Voters who fail to reveal are excluded from the tally.
- Sepolia is a public testnet and is not a production election environment.
- A formal security audit, institutional multisig, privacy review, and operational runbook would be required before real-world use.
